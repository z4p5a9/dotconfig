---
description: Use this when you want to use the `web-surfer` agent to find latest, up-to-date, and modern information from the web.
---

Spawn one or more `web-surfer` sub-agents to research the web and return concise answers with links and relevant evidence.

Use it for web-backed questions, recent information, official sources, industry standards, practices, comparisons, source quotes, publication dates, and gaps in your training.

Give each `web-surfer` a specific research target. If you need to research separate targets or unrelated angles, spawn `web-surfer` agents in parallel with one target each.
